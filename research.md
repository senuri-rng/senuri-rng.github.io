### Research Focus: FDA on Diffeomorphic Manifolds

I specialize in Functional Data Analysis (FDA) on diffeomorphic manifolds for medical imaging. My primary research introduces a Finite Element Method (FEM)-based FDA framework to model spatially registered color images, specifically applied to extracting spatial biomarkers for non-invasive disease screening.

My work focuses on providing a mathematically rigorous, transparent statistical alternative to black-box Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) in clinical settings. By establishing diffeomorphic correspondence across patient-specific tissue contours, this methodology allows for functional analysis directly on the resulting manifold to reliably predict clinical outcomes.
